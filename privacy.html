Summary

Pixel Baseball Derby does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Information We Collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves your best score on your device so it is there the next time you play. This value never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Third Parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services, and we do not sell or share any information.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.
