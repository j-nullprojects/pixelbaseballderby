How to Play

Watch the pitch and swipe across the screen to swing. Only home runs count. Any swing that is not a home run is an out. Taking a pitch costs nothing. Hit as many home runs as you can before you run out of outs.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 15.0 or later. The game plays in landscape.

Common Questions

How do I reset my best score?
Your best score is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. The game works fully offline.
